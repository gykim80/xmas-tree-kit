    How adoption works

    1. 01. Pick your tree

      Choose a size and a look. Each tree has a name and a row number on the farm.

    2. 02. We decorate and deliver

      Lights, garland and the ornaments you chose go on at the farm. Delivery is the first week of December.

    3. 03. We replant in January

      We collect the tree after New Year and plant it back in its row. You can adopt the same tree next year.

    Hang your own ornaments

    Click the tree to hang an ornament. Shift-click one to take it off. We hang the same ornaments on your tree before it leaves the farm.
